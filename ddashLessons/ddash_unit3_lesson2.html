🎯 Learning Objective

Find common multiples, lowest common multiples (LCM), and sort numbers using Venn diagrams

📐 Key Concepts

Multiple: A number you get by multiplying another number by 1, 2, 3, 4 … (e.g. multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 …)
Common multiple: A number that is a multiple of two or more numbers (e.g. 12 is a common multiple of 3 and 4)
LCM (Lowest Common Multiple): The smallest common multiple (e.g. LCM of 3 and 4 is 12)
