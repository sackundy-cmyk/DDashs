🎯 Learning Objective

Count on and back in large number sequences, and find missing numbers in number patterns

📐 Key Concept: Sequences

A sequence is a list of numbers that follow a rule. Look at neighbouring numbers to find the pattern — then use it to fill in the missing ones.

Find the rule: Subtract any two neighbouring numbers to find the step size.
Count on: The numbers get bigger — add the step each time.
Count back: The numbers get smaller — subtract the step each time.
Example — what comes next?
281 459→ 281 460→ 281 461→ 281 462→ ?→ ? Step = +1 → answers: 281 463, 281 464
Example — find the missing number
389 450→ 389 452→ ?→ 389 456 Step = +2 → missing: 389 454
