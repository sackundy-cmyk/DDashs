📐 Key Concept: Rules of Divisibility

A number is divisible by another if there is no remainder. Use these quick rules to test — no long division needed!

÷ 2 — Last digit is even (0, 2, 4, 6, 8)
÷ 3 — Sum of digits is divisible by 3
÷ 4 — Last two digits divisible by 4
÷ 5 — Last digit is 0 or 5
÷ 6 — Divisible by both 2 AND 3
÷ 9 — Sum of digits is divisible by 9
÷ 10 — Last digit is 0
÷ 7 & 8 — Divide directly to check
