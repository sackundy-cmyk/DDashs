🎯 Learning Objective

Read, write and identify digits in decimal numbers up to thousandths — like 3.275

📐 Key Concept: Thousandths

We can extend place value one step further past hundredths. The thousandths place is the third digit after the decimal point.

Example: 3.275

💡 3.275: Units=3 · Tenths=2 (=2/10) · Hundredths=7 (=7/100) · Thousandths=5 (=5/1000)
🔑 Thousandths = 3 decimal places. The fraction always has 1000 on the bottom.
More examples:
7/1000 = 0.007  ·  45/1000 = 0.045  ·  325/1000 = 0.325  ·  12 800/1000 = 12.800
