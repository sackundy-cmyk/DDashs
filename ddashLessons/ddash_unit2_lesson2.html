🎯 Learning Objective

Read negative numbers on number lines and order positive and negative temperatures

📐 Key Concept: Negative Numbers

Numbers less than zero are called negative numbers. They are written with a minus sign, like −5 or −12.

On a number line: negative numbers sit to the left of zero. The further left, the smaller the number.
−10 is smaller than −2 — because −10 is further from zero.
Ordering temperatures: −11°C is colder (lower) than −4°C, which is colder than 0°C.
