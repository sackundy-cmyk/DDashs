🎯 Learning Objective

Solve one-step and two-step equations using inverse operations

📐 Key Concept: Solving Equations

To solve an equation, use the inverse (opposite) operation to isolate the unknown.

Inverse operations:
+ and − are inverses  ·  × and ÷ are inverses
One step: n + 5 = 12 → n = 12 − 5 = 7
Two steps: 3n + 2 = 14 → first 14 − 2 = 12, then 12 ÷ 3 = 4
