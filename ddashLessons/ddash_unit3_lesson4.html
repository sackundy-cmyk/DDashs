🎯 Learning Objective

Identify prime numbers up to 100, calculate square numbers, and spot non-square numbers

📐 Key Concepts

Prime number: Has exactly 2 factors — 1 and itself. (e.g. 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13…)
⚠️ 1 is NOT prime — it has only one factor.   2 is the only even prime.

Square number: A whole number multiplied by itself. (e.g. 6 × 6 = 6² = 36)
Square numbers up to 100: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100
