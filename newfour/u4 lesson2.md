## 4. Answer Key

### Section 5 – Comparison of Expressions

First, evaluate both sides:

| Item | Left Expression | Left Value | Right Expression | Right Value | Comparison |
|------|----------------|------------|------------------|-------------|------------|
| a) | (4×5)+(12÷4) = 20 + 3 = 23 | 23 | (8+6)+(3×3) = 14 + 9 = 23 | 23 | = |
| b) | 24−(8×2)+4 = 24−16+4 = 12 | 12 | (4×5)−(3×4) = 20−12 = 8 | 8 | > |
| c) | (4×6)−(14÷7)+8 = 24−2+8 = 30 | 30 | 6×(18÷3)−4 = 6×6−4 = 36−4 = 32 | 32 | < |
| d) | (8+14)−(6×3) = 22−18 = 4 | 4 | (6+9)÷(28−23) = 15÷5 = 3 | 3 | > |

**Answers:**
a) =  
b) >  
c) <  
d) >  

### Section 6 – Brackets for Largest Possible Answer

For each expression, try different bracket placements and compute:

**a) 8 + 4 − 2 × 3**
- No brackets (BODMAS): 8+4−6 = 6
- (8+4)−(2×3) = 12−6 = 6
- 8+(4−2)×3 = 8+2×3 = 8+6 = 14
- (8+4−2)×3 = 10×3 = 30  ← **largest**
- 8+4−(2×3) = 12−6 = 6
**Answer:** (8 + 4 − 2) × 3

**b) 9 × 3 − 1 + 5**
- No brackets: 27−1+5 = 31
- (9×3)−(1+5) = 27−6 = 21
- 9×(3−1)+5 = 9×2+5 = 18+5 = 23
- 9×(3−1+5) = 9×7 = 63  ← **largest**
- (9×3−1)+5 = 26+5 = 31
**Answer:** 9 × (3 − 1 + 5)

**c) 6 + 8 × 4 ÷ 2**
- No brackets (×÷ left to right): 6 + 32÷2 = 6+16 = 22
- (6+8)×4÷2 = 14×4÷2 = 56÷2 = 28
- 6+(8×4)÷2 = 6+32÷2 = 6+16 = 22
- 6+8×(4÷2) = 6+8×2 = 6+16 = 22
- (6+8×4)÷2 = (6+32)÷2 = 38÷2 = 19
- (6+8)×(4÷2) = 14×2 = 28
- (6+8×4÷2) same as 22
**Largest is 28** from (6+8)×4÷2 or (6+8)×(4÷2). Typically choose: **(6 + 8) × 4 ÷ 2**

**d) 10 ÷ 2 + 3 × 7**
- No brackets: 5 + 21 = 26
- (10÷2)+(3×7) = 5+21 = 26
- 10÷(2+3)×7 = 10÷5×7 = 2×7 = 14
- (10÷2+3)×7 = (5+3)×7 = 8×7 = 56  ← **largest**
- 10÷(2+3×7) = 10÷(2+21) = 10÷23 ≈ 0.43
**Answer:** (10 ÷ 2 + 3) × 7

**e) 6 − 2 × 8 + 20**
- No brackets: 6−16+20 = 10
- (6−2)×8+20 = 4×8+20 = 32+20 = 52
- 6−(2×8)+20 = 6−16+20 = 10
- 6−2×(8+20) = 6−2×28 = 6−56 = −50
- (6−2×8)+20 = (6−16)+20 = −10+20 = 10
- 6−(2×8+20) = 6−(16+20) = 6−36 = −30
**Largest is 52** from **(6 − 2) × 8 + 20**

**f) 8 + 10 ÷ 2 × 3**
- No brackets (÷× left to right): 8 + 5×3 = 8+15 = 23
- (8+10)÷2×3 = 18÷2×3 = 9×3 = 27
- 8+(10÷2)×3 = 8+5×3 = 8+15 = 23
- 8+10÷(2×3) = 8+10÷6 ≈ 8+1.67 = 9.67
- (8+10÷2)×3 = (8+5)×3 = 13×3 = 39  ← **largest**
**Answer:** (8 + 10 ÷ 2) × 3
