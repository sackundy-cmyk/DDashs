## 4. Answer Key

### Section 1 – Multiplication
a) 38 × 24 = **912**  
b) 56 × 56 = **3136**  
c) 48 × 80 = **3840**  
d) 234 × 62 = **14508**  
e) 387 × 46 = **17802**  
f) 39 × 493 = **19227**  

### Section 2 – Cost Comparison
**Item Prices:**
| Item          | Price  |
|---------------|--------|
| Bookmark      | $4.95  |
| Badge         | $9.54  |
| Statue        | $2.34  |
| Vase          | $9.32  |
| T‑shirt       | $6.87  |
| Baseball cap  | $12.52 |
| Apron         | $15.80 |
| Cup           | $5.84  |

**Calculations:**
a) 4 bookmarks = 4 × 4.95 = $19.80; 8 badges = 8 × 9.54 = $76.32 → **8 badges cost more**  
b) 3 statues = 3 × 2.34 = $7.02; 5 vases = 5 × 9.32 = $46.60 → **5 vases cost more**  
c) 4 t‑shirts = 4 × 6.87 = $27.48; 6 caps = 6 × 12.52 = $75.12 → **6 baseball caps cost more**  
d) 3 aprons = 3 × 15.80 = $47.40; 7 cups = 7 × 5.84 = $40.88 → **3 aprons cost more**  

**Answers:**
a) 8 badges  
b) 5 vases  
c) 6 baseball caps  
d) 3 aprons
