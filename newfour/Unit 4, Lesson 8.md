## 2. Interactive Element Determination

| Section | Interaction Type |
|---------|------------------|
| 1 (a–f) | Drag‑and‑drop digits and decimal point to complete column addition with two decimal places (hundredths). |
| 2 (a–f) | Drag‑and‑drop digits and decimal point to build answers; students align decimal points mentally. |

Both sections use the standard D‑DASH digit card palette (0–9, `.`, DEL). Answer slots accommodate up to 4 digits plus decimal point. Questions grouped in pairs with a "Check" button per group.
